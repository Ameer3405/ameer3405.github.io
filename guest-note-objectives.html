The key Benefits & Objectives of this Initiative (my first thoughts)

  • Engaging our Scholarly Community:

    • This event will bring together our scholarly community, including professors and students. I am working with Biology and Sustainable Science professors to plan a special session focused on Natural Resources Management and Climate Change. The session will be aimed at students in the Environment and Ecology class, as well as those studying Physics and Sustainable Science. This special opportunity will enable our community to explore and discuss lessons about the fair distribution of resources among indigenous communities, allowing us to gain valuable insights through comparison and contrast.

  • Promoting Knowledge Sharing and Cross-Cultural Understanding:

    • The event provides a valuable opportunity for sharing knowledge and experiences between our academic setting and the broader Hawaii community. It helps people from different cultures understand each other better and work together. This makes our Maui community more aware of global issues and expands the reach of education beyond traditional boundaries.

  • Enhancing Academic Rigor and International Education Partnership:

    • This event aligns with our commitment to academic rigor and strengthens our international education partnerships. It provides a platform to share extensive knowledge and insights on critical subjects like natural resources management. It's a chance to talk about important topics like taking care of nature and the changes in weather.
