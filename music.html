Melodies of My Heart

Music gives a soul to the universe, wings to the mind, flight to the imagination, and life to everyone. ~Plato

When I feel lost, I stop for a moment, play my harmonium, and let the music lead me back to where I belong!

Music expresses emotions that cannot be expressed fully in words or in any language! Music is eternal.
